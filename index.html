GPA Calculator

Student grade and GPA computation system

How to use this calculator

  • Enter your subject grade point in the first box.
  • Enter the credit value of that subject in the second box.
  • Click “Add Subject” to add more subjects.
  • Click “Calculate GPA” to get your final GPA.

How GPA is calculated

GPA is calculated using the standard weighted average formula:

GPA = (Grade Point × Credit) ÷ Total Credits

The calculator adds all grade points multiplied by their credits and divides the result by the total credits.
